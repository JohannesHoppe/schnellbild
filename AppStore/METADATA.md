**Name:** Schnellbild

**Subtitle:** Photo & video viewer, fast

Open a folder, see thumbnails, page through with the keyboard. Videos play right away, MKV, AVI and WebM too. No import, no subscription, no account, no tracking.

**Description:**
Schnellbild shows pictures and videos without importing anything: open a folder, see thumbnails, look at one picture large, page through with the keyboard. It stays quick on big folders and network shares alike.

THIS VERSION SUPPORTS THE PROJECT
Schnellbild is open source and free to download on GitHub. This is the same app. Buying it here supports its development. Thank you!

PICTURES
• JPEG, HEIC, AVIF, JPEG XL, PNG, GIF, WebP, TIFF, PSD, OpenEXR, SVG, PDF and RAW photos of the cameras macOS knows
• Grid, list and full view, slideshow through all subfolders
• Zoom, rotate, auto enhance (exposure, contrast, saturation, sharpness), for viewing only: your files stay as they are
• File info with format, codecs and bitrate; a note when a file's extension doesn't match its content

VIDEOS

ARCHIVES AS FOLDERS
• ZIP, RAR and 7z open like folders, comic books (CBZ, CBR) included, also with passwords and in parts

SORT LIKE IN NORTON COMMANDER
• Two panes side by side: mark, copy with F5, move with F6

• Search folder and file names through all subfolders

## Review notes (English)

Schnellbild is an image and video viewer. It runs in the App Sandbox and reads only the folders the user chooses ("Open Folder…", ⌘O) or drops on its window, plus Pictures, Movies and Downloads. No account, no network access, no in-app purchases. The same app is free as a download on GitHub (open source, MIT); this App Store version lets users support the project. It embeds VLCKit (LGPL 2.1, built without VLC's GPL modules) to play formats AVFoundation can't; the license agreement allows what the LGPL asks (changing and debugging that library). To try it: open the Pictures folder, press Space on a picture, the arrow keys to page, F1 for all keys.
