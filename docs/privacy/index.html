Privacy policy

Schnellbild is an image and video viewer for macOS by HAUS HOPPE - ITS (Johannes Hoppe); the full address is in the legal notice. This policy covers the app, as downloaded from GitHub and as bought in the Mac App Store (both are the same app), and this website.

The app

Schnellbild collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet: the App Sandbox it runs in gives it no network access at all.

What stays on your Mac, and only there:

  • Settings (the last folder, sort order, bookmarks, volume …) in the app's own preferences.
  • Folder access: the folders you opened to Schnellbild, as macOS bookmarks, so it can show them again after a restart. Settings → Folder access forgets them.
  • Archive passwords you typed, in your login keychain (entry "Schnellbild archive passwords"); Settings → Forget Saved Passwords removes them.
  • Thumbnails, in macOS's own thumbnail cache.

Schnellbild reads your pictures, videos and archives only to show them to you. It never sends them, their names or anything about them anywhere. Apple may collect data about the download or purchase under its own privacy policy; Schnellbild gets none of it.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no content from other servers (no web fonts, no embedded videos). It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes; see GitHub Pages: data collection and the GitHub privacy statement. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The download of the app comes from GitHub as well.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since neither the app nor this website keeps personal data of its own, there is usually nothing on our side to look up or delete.
