What it does

Look at it large, page with the keys

Space opens a picture as large as the window allows, ← → page through the folder. Zoom in with the trackpad or + −, turn with ⌥← ⌥→, full screen with f. Animated GIFs, SVG and PDF included.

A slideshow (s) runs through the folder and all its subfolders, photos and videos alike.

Videos, also the ones macOS can't play

What macOS plays itself (MP4, MOV, AVCHD …) plays through macOS, everything else through the VLC engine built in: MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, MPEG, DVD video.

  • ← → jump a tenth of the video, ⇧← ⇧→ a hundredth
  • ↑ ↓ set the volume, for every video
  • Finder and Quick Look get thumbnails for MKV, WebM, WMV, FLV and VOB too

Archives open like folders

ZIP, RAR and 7z, comics (CBZ, CBR) included, also in several parts; ZIP and RAR also with a password. Nothing is unpacked to disk, and videos play straight from the archive.

Sort your holiday photos like in Norton Commander

D shows two folders side by side. Mark pictures, then F5 copies and F6 moves them to the other side, or drag them there.

Rename, new folder, trash, bookmarks, and undo for renaming, trashing, copying and moving.

Search through every subfolder

The search field matches the names of folders, archives, pictures and videos, in the folder shown and everything below it. Results arrive while it searches, a slow network share included. Ignoring case, matching it, or a regular expression.

Better pictures, files untouched

E improves exposure, contrast, saturation and sharpness automatically, for photos and videos alike. A opens the sliders. It is a view: your files stay as they are.

What the file really is

I shows the format, resolution, codecs, bitrate, bit depth, colour profile and camera, and a note when the file's extension doesn't match its content.

Every key at hand

F1 lists every key, to print too; K shows a small keyboard to click. Grid or list (L), sort by name, kind, date or size, several windows, and a boss key (X).

Safe with files from others

Pictures, videos and archives from strangers are exactly what a viewer is for, so Schnellbild treats every file as hostile, out of the box.

  • In Apple's App Sandbox. No network at all, starts no programs by itself, sees only the folders you open to it plus Pictures, Movies and Downloads.
  • Only reading. Looking at something never changes it and never unpacks it to disk.
  • Videos can't go online. A "video" that is really a playlist pointing to the internet plays nothing.
  • Double click opens documents, not programs. Apps, scripts and installers never, even when they hide behind a picture's name.
  • Signed and notarized, with Apple's Hardened Runtime.

Coming from Phiewer, IINA or Norton Commander?

They were the models: Phiewer for browsing pictures by keyboard, IINA for handling videos quickly, Norton Commander for the dual pane. How Schnellbild compares: a free Phiewer alternative, a free IINA alternative for looking through videos.
