A free VLC alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of VLC?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. Schnellbild and VLC side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where VLC is stronger

  • plays nearly every format, discs (DVD/Blu-ray), network streams and capture devices
  • runs on every desktop and mobile OS
  • subtitles from files and online (VLSub), audio tracks, speed, filters, converting/streaming out
  • saved playlists, music, visualizations
  • fully remappable hotkeys

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Undo renames, moves, trash
  • Runs in Apple's App Sandbox
  • Finder and Quick Look thumbnails for MKV and more
