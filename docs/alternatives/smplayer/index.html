A free SMPlayer alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of SMPlayer?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. Schnellbild and SMPlayer side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where SMPlayer is stronger

  • Resumes every file with its own settings (audio, subtitles, position)
  • YouTube playback and online subtitle download
  • Saved playlists, favorites, TV/radio lists
  • Skins and icon themes, 30+ languages
  • Runs on Windows and Linux too

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
  • Undo renames, moves, trash
  • Never goes online
