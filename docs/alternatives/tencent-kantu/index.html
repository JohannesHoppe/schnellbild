A free 看图 Kantu alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of 看图 Kantu?

Schnellbild has no library: it shows the photos and videos where they lie on disk, with the date taken and the place from their metadata, and nothing is uploaded. Schnellbild and 看图 Kantu side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where 看图 Kantu is stronger

  • Face recognition, places map, duplicates finder, curated albums
  • Imports straight from phones, cameras and PTP devices
  • Ratings and filters for big files

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Auto enhance and sliders without changing the file
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys
  • Open source
  • Never goes online
