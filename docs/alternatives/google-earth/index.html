A free Google Earth alternative for your own photos

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of Google Earth?

Schnellbild shows where your photos and videos were taken, on a map and a globe inside the app, without going online. For satellite imagery, 3D cities and Street View, Google Earth stays the place to go.

What Schnellbild offers

  • Every photo and video of a folder and its subfolders on the map, placed by its GPS data: from the camera or phone, a GoPro, a DJI drone, a Sony camera, an XMP sidecar or a GPX track in the folder
  • A globe in front of the stars, with day and night if you like and the cities lit on the night side; zoom in and it flattens into the map
  • A timeline of the trip: drag across it to see only that part
  • A tour (T) that glides from place to place, shows the photos large and plays the videos for a few seconds
  • The map is inside the app: it works without internet, and no location leaves your Mac
  • Free, no account, in Apple's App Sandbox

What Schnellbild doesn't do

  • No satellite or aerial imagery: the map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data
  • No 3D buildings and no Street View
  • No search for places or addresses; for sorting by distance it takes coordinates or a map link
  • No historical imagery and no GIS tools
