Schnellbild and Apple Preview side by side, fairly: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

What Apple Preview is good at

Apple's built-in viewer for PDFs and images on every Mac. Strong at PDFs, annotations, signing and quick image fixes like crop, resize and format conversion.

Where Apple Preview is stronger

  • Already installed on every Mac
  • Full PDF toolset: annotate, sign forms, combine, password-protect
  • Image editing: crop, resize, background removal, batch conversion
  • Opens 3D files

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Browses folders directly, no import
  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
