IrfanView for Mac? Here is the alternative

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of IrfanView?

Schnellbild opens a folder straight away, with nothing to import, and you page through its pictures with the arrow keys: HEIC, RAW, PSD, animated GIF and PDF included, with the folder's videos in the same grid. You made it to the Mac. The photos came along, the muscle memory came along, only IrfanView stayed behind on the old PC. It doesn't have to stay that way.

What IrfanView fans like, and how it works in Schnellbild

  • Page through with the keyboard: Space opens a picture, ← → page through the folder.
  • Browses folders directly, no import: Open a folder with ⌘O or drop it on the window.
  • Thumbnail grid with folder previews: Folders show their first pictures; folders without any are greyed out.
  • HEIC, AVIF, JPEG XL, WebP: Through macOS's own ImageIO.
  • Camera RAW: The RAW formats macOS knows, developed by macOS.
  • Animated GIF, PNG, WebP: They play, and zoom and turn like any picture.
  • Zoom, pan, rotate (view only): + − or a pinch to zoom, ⌥← ⌥→ to turn; the file stays as it is.
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV: Through the built-in VLC engine.

Where IrfanView stays ahead

  • Huge format list, including rare and retro formats
  • Batch conversion and batch rename
  • Image editing, paint plug-in, effects, Photoshop 8BF filters
  • Scanning, printing, OCR plug-in
  • Search by EXIF/IPTC and text, not only names
  • Slideshows saved as EXE, screensaver or MP4
  • Runs on very old Windows versions and from a USB stick

Left out on purpose

  • Saved playlists: Folders instead of playlists: the slideshow makes the list.
  • Audio files, music mode: Pictures and videos only; audio files open in their own app.
  • Edits images (crop, resize, save): Shows pictures without changing them: your files stay as they are.
  • Batch convert or rename: Converting and renaming in batches is left to other apps.
  • Cloud sync, sharing online: Never goes online, on principle.
