A free OmniPlayer alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of OmniPlayer?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. Schnellbild and OmniPlayer side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where OmniPlayer is stronger

  • Online subtitle search
  • Playback speed down to 0.1x, frame by frame
  • Casting to TVs (DLNA, Chromecast, Roku)
  • YouTube/Vimeo URLs, SMB/FTP sources
  • Audio equalizer, music playback
  • Picture adjustments and GIF export

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
  • Undo renames, moves, trash
