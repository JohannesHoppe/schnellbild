What VidHub is good at

Infuse-style video library and player for Apple devices: it scans local folders, NAS, cloud drives and Emby/Jellyfin/Plex servers, builds a poster library from TMDB and syncs progress over iCloud. One of the most-rated media players in the Mac App Store (2.8K ratings, US).

Where VidHub is stronger

  • Movie/TV library with posters and metadata
  • iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac
  • Cloud drives, NAS, Emby/Jellyfin/Plex
  • Online subtitle search
  • Casting via DLNA, Chromecast, Roku, AirPlay

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
  • Open source
