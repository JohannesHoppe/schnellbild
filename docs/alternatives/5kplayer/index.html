A free 5KPlayer alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of 5KPlayer?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. Schnellbild and 5KPlayer side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where 5KPlayer is stronger

  • AirPlay receiver and screen-mirror recording
  • DLNA server/renderer
  • Online video downloader (YouTube etc.)
  • DVD and ISO playback
  • Music library, radio, IPTV
  • Playback speed, video cutter, MP3 extraction

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Browses folders directly, no import
  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
