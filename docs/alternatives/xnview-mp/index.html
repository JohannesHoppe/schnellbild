A free XnView MP alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of XnView MP?

Schnellbild has no library: it shows the photos and videos where they lie on disk, with the date taken and the place from their metadata, and nothing is uploaded. Schnellbild and XnView MP side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where XnView MP is stronger

  • More than 500 image formats, export to about 70
  • Batch convert and batch rename with regex
  • Catalog with categories, ratings and color labels
  • Metadata editing (EXIF, IPTC, XMP)
  • Image editing, compare up to 4 images, duplicate finder
  • Runs on Windows and Linux too

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Undo renames, moves, trash
  • Open source
  • Never goes online
  • Finder and Quick Look thumbnails for MKV and more
  • Notes a file whose extension doesn't match its content
  • Boss key (hide everything at once)

What XnView MP is good at

XnView MP is a long-running cross-platform image browser and organizer that reads more than 500 formats. It combines a file browser, viewer, catalog with categories and ratings, batch converter and basic editor in one app. On the Mac it is a Qt app, free for private use.
