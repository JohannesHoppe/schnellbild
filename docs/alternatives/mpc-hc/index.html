MPC-HC for Mac? Here is the alternative

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of MPC-HC?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. New Mac, same videos, and MPC-HC is the one thing that didn't make the move. Your MKV files are still waiting to be played.

What MPC-HC fans like, and how it works in Schnellbild

  • Page through with the keyboard: Space opens a picture, ← → page through the folder.
  • Browses folders directly, no import: Open a folder with ⌘O or drop it on the window.
  • Zoom, pan, rotate (view only): + − or a pinch to zoom, ⌥← ⌥→ to turn; the file stays as it is.
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV: Through the built-in VLC engine.
  • Subtitles from inside the video: V turns them on and off, ⌃⌘S picks the track; switched on halfway, the running line shows at once, and ASS keeps its styles.
  • Choose the audio track: ⌃⌘A or the control bar.
  • Playback speed: < and > from 0.5× to 2×, the voices keep their pitch.
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys: ← → jump a tenth of the video, with ⇧ a hundredth.

Where MPC-HC stays ahead

  • very light and quick to start
  • renderers and shaders for enthusiasts (madVR, MPC VR)
  • subtitle search and download, yt-dlp streaming
  • boss key, frame stepping, many configurable keys

Left out on purpose

  • Loads subtitle files lying next to the video: Reads only the video itself, nothing lying next to it.
  • Saved playlists: Folders instead of playlists: the slideshow makes the list.
  • Audio files, music mode: Pictures and videos only; audio files open in their own app.
  • Plays streams and addresses from the internet: Never goes online, on principle.
  • Downloads subtitles from the internet: Never goes online, on principle.
