A free Finder & Quick Look alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of Finder & Quick Look?

Schnellbild has no library: it shows the photos and videos where they lie on disk, with the date taken and the place from their metadata, and nothing is uploaded. Schnellbild and Finder & Quick Look side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Schnellbild isn't a complete file manager: no FTP, SFTP or cloud connections, no folder sync, no packing of archives. It is made for looking through photos and videos and sorting them: thumbnails of everything, two panes side by side with F5 to copy and F6 to move, archives open like folders.

Where Finder & Quick Look is stronger

  • Built into macOS, works for nearly every file type
  • Tags, Smart Folders, Spotlight search
  • Batch rename and Quick Actions
  • iCloud Drive and AirDrop

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
  • Auto enhance and sliders without changing the file
