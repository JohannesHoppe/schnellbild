A free Xee³ alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of Xee³?

Schnellbild opens CBZ, CBR, ZIP, RAR and 7z like folders, without unpacking anything to disk, and you page through them with the arrow keys. Schnellbild and Xee³ side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where Xee³ is stronger

  • Exotic legacy formats (PCX, IFF, MAG, PVR, XBM)
  • JPEG creator identification from quantization tables
  • PSD layers view
  • Lossless JPEG crop

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Free, every feature
  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Search names through all subfolders
  • Auto enhance and sliders without changing the file
