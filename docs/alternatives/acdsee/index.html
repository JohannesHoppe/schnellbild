A free ACDSee alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of ACDSee?

Schnellbild has no library: it shows the photos and videos where they lie on disk, with the date taken and the place from their metadata, and nothing is uploaded. Schnellbild and ACDSee side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where ACDSee is stronger

  • Full DAM: ratings, keywords, categories, faces, AI keywords and image similarity
  • Non-destructive RAW editing with AI masking
  • Batch rename, resize, convert, metadata
  • Duplicate finder
  • Generative AI editing
  • Windows: ZIP/RAR browsing and video metadata

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Free, every feature
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Open source
  • Never goes online

Long-standing photo DAM and RAW editor that works on existing folders without import: catalog database, ratings, keywords, AI faces and keywords, non-destructive Develop mode and batch tools. The Windows editions also play common videos and browse ZIP/RAR archives; the Mac edition is more limited there. Paid, aimed at photographers who manage and edit.
