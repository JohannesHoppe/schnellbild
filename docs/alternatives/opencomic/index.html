A free OpenComic alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of OpenComic?

Schnellbild opens CBZ, CBR, ZIP, RAR and 7z like folders, without unpacking anything to disk, and you page through them with the arrow keys. Schnellbild and OpenComic side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where OpenComic is stronger

  • Many formats incl. EPUB, MOBI, SVG, JPEG XL, ACE/LZH archives
  • Manga, webtoon, double page and guided view
  • Direct SMB/FTP/SFTP/WebDAV/S3 and OPDS
  • AI upscaling, descreen and artifact removal
  • Tabs and multiple windows; custom shortcuts and gamepad

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys
  • Undo renames, moves, trash
