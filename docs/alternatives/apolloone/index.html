What ApolloOne is good at

Fast photo viewer and culling tool for photographers that works on folders without imports. Strong on RAW, HDR, metadata, ratings, AF points and batch work; many features are spread over three paid editions.

Where ApolloOne is stronger

  • Culling: picks, ratings, Focus Score, AI rating, stacks
  • HDR RAW display and HDR export
  • EXIF/IPTC/XMP editing, keywords, GPS maps
  • Memory card import
  • External SRT subtitles
  • AppleScript automation

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Open source
  • Never goes online
