A free nPlayer alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of nPlayer?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. Schnellbild and nPlayer side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where nPlayer is stronger

  • Network protocols (SMB, NFS, SFTP, WebDAV, UPnP)
  • Advanced subtitles (styling, sync, SMI, SUP)
  • A-B repeat, language-learning 'Listening Drill'
  • Picture in Picture, playback speed
  • Audio equalizer and sync

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Free, every feature
  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed
