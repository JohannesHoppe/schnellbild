A free Google Photos alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of Google Photos?

Schnellbild has no library: it shows the photos and videos where they lie on disk, with the date taken and the place from their metadata, and nothing is uploaded. Schnellbild and Google Photos side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where Google Photos is stronger

  • Automatic backup from Android and iPhone
  • Search by people, things and places
  • Sharing albums and partner sharing
  • Access from any browser

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Browses folders directly, no import
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys
  • Open source
  • Never goes online
