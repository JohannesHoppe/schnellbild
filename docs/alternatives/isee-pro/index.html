A free iSee Pro alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of iSee Pro?

Schnellbild opens a folder straight away, with nothing to import, and you page through its pictures with the arrow keys: HEIC, RAW, PSD, animated GIF and PDF included, with the folder's videos in the same grid. Schnellbild and iSee Pro side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where iSee Pro is stronger

  • Very cheap ($0.99 Pro) and tiny (4.4 MB)
  • Remembers archive passwords
  • Search by image dimensions and Draw Things parameters
  • Live wallpaper/desktop features

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Photos and videos together in one grid
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Auto enhance and sliders without changing the file
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys
  • Open source

What iSee Pro is good at

PriceFree with IAP: Pro $0.99
Version11.5
Last update2025-05-30
PlatformsmacOS

iSee Pro is a small Mac App Store image browser that loads all images of a folder and its subfolders with prefetching for fast paging. It has list, thumbnail and full-size views, name/size/date search, EXIF overlay, a magnifier and opens ZIP/RAR archives, remembering their passwords. Requires macOS 12.
