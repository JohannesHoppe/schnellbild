A free Apple Photos alternative for Mac

Schnellbild and Apple Photos side by side, fairly: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

PriceFree (included with macOS); iCloud+ storage for iCloud Photos is paid
VersionPhotos 12.0 on macOS Golden Gate 27.2.1
Last update2026-11-09 (macOS 27.2.1)
PlatformsmacOS, iOS, iPadOS, visionOS

What Apple Photos is good at

Apple's photo library with iCloud sync across devices. Good for organising and editing a personal photo collection, with people recognition, memories and non-destructive edits.

Where Apple Photos is stronger

  • iCloud sync with iPhone and iPad
  • Non-destructive editing of photos and videos
  • People, places and content search
  • Memories, slideshows with music, shared albums

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Browses folders directly, no import
  • Plays MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Two panes side by side (F5 copy, F6 move)
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys
