A free digiKam alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of digiKam?

Schnellbild has no library: it shows the photos and videos where they lie on disk, with the date taken and the place from their metadata, and nothing is uploaded. Schnellbild and digiKam side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where digiKam is stronger

  • Database with tags, labels, face recognition, similarity and duplicate search, map and timeline search
  • Natural language and advanced search across all collections
  • Full image editor and Batch Queue Manager with versioning
  • RAW processing via LibRaw, 16-bit and colour management
  • Video playback speed control and frame stepping
  • Many export targets (Dropbox, OneDrive, Flickr, Piwigo, HTML galleries) and DLNA
  • Free, open source, runs on Linux and Windows too

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR
  • Plays videos straight from archives
  • Skim videos with the arrow keys
  • Never goes online

digiKam is a full open-source photo management suite from KDE for large collections. It indexes folders into a database and offers tags, face recognition, geolocation, similarity search, a capable image editor, batch processing and many export targets.
