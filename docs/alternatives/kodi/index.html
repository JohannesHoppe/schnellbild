A free Kodi alternative for Mac

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Download for freeSchnellbild.dmg Mac App Storesoon, €0.99 to support it

Schnellbild instead of Kodi?

Schnellbild isn't a full media player. It shows the videos of a folder as thumbnails next to the photos, plays MKV, AVI and WebM, and the arrow keys skim through a video a tenth at a time. Schnellbild and Kodi side by side: what each does better, then every feature with a source.

Where Kodi is stronger

  • media center for the TV with remote control, libraries with posters and metadata
  • music library and player
  • streaming and subtitle add-ons, PVR/live TV
  • RAR passwords and multi-part RAR via add-on
  • runs on TV boxes, Android, Linux, Raspberry Pi

Where Schnellbild is stronger

  • Never goes online
  • Runs in Apple's App Sandbox
  • Finder and Quick Look thumbnails for MKV and more
  • Boss key (hide everything at once)
