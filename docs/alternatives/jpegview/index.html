JPEGView for Mac? Here is the alternative

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of JPEGView?

Schnellbild opens a folder straight away, with nothing to import, and you page through its pictures with the arrow keys: HEIC, RAW, PSD, animated GIF and PDF included, with the folder's videos in the same grid. You made it to the Mac. The photos came along, the muscle memory came along, only JPEGView stayed behind on the old PC. It doesn't have to stay that way.

What JPEGView fans like, and how it works in Schnellbild

  • Page through with the keyboard: Space opens a picture, ← → page through the folder.
  • Browses folders directly, no import: Open a folder with ⌘O or drop it on the window.
  • HEIC, AVIF, JPEG XL, WebP: Through macOS's own ImageIO.
  • Camera RAW: The RAW formats macOS knows, developed by macOS.
  • Animated GIF, PNG, WebP: They play, and zoom and turn like any picture.
  • Zoom, pan, rotate (view only): + − or a pinch to zoom, ⌥← ⌥→ to turn; the file stays as it is.
  • Slideshow: S starts it, Space pauses.
  • Slideshow through all subfolders, photos and videos mixed: Every subfolder, photos and videos mixed, in your sort order.

Where JPEGView stays ahead

  • Real-time automatic colour, contrast and local density correction while viewing
  • Extremely small and portable (no install)
  • Fully remappable keys via KeyMap.txt
  • Lossless JPEG crop/rotate
  • Image series played as a movie at a set frame rate

Left out on purpose

  • Saved playlists: Folders instead of playlists: the slideshow makes the list.
  • Edits images (crop, resize, save): Shows pictures without changing them: your files stay as they are.
  • Batch convert or rename: Converting and renaming in batches is left to other apps.
