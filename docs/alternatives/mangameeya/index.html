MangaMeeya for Mac? Here is the alternative

Schnellbild is a free Mac app for the pictures and videos in your folders: open a folder and its thumbnails are there, one key shows a picture large. It plays MKV, AVI and WebM, opens ZIP and RAR archives like folders, and works without an account and without network access.

Schnellbild instead of MangaMeeya?

Schnellbild opens CBZ, CBR, ZIP, RAR and 7z like folders, without unpacking anything to disk, and you page through them with the arrow keys. Your comics made the trip to the Mac without a complaint. MangaMeeya didn't come along.

What MangaMeeya fans like, and how it works in Schnellbild

  • Page through with the keyboard: Space opens a picture, ← → page through the folder.
  • Browses folders directly, no import: Open a folder with ⌘O or drop it on the window.
  • Thumbnail grid with folder previews: Folders show their first pictures; folders without any are greyed out.
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z open like folders: Opened like folders, nothing unpacked to disk.
  • Comic books CBZ, CBR: CBZ and CBR open like folders; page with ← →.
  • Slideshow: S starts it, Space pauses.
  • Everything by keyboard: Every action has a key.
  • Your own keyboard shortcuts: Set your own keys, for free, with a preset as in Phiewer.

Where MangaMeeya stays ahead

  • Familiar manga workflow for long-time users
  • Tabs and split windows over two monitors
